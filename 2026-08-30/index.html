What to keep—and what to watch

Observations from a complete Monday–Sunday window; not a diagnosis.

Working well

  • Training consistency: seven traditional strength sessions exceeded the established roughly five-times-weekly routine.
  • Movement variety: 37 workouts combined strength, 25 walks, four table-tennis sessions, and one functional-strength session.
  • Stable core signals: resting heart rate stayed 60–66 bpm, weight measured 66.32–66.86 kg, and VO₂ max was 43.66 on Aug 27.
  • Recovery finish: HRV rebounded from 32.52 ms on Aug 29 to 46.79 ms on Aug 30, the week's highest reading.
  • Protein consistency: daily intake stayed between 167.7 and 195.7 g, averaging 176.1 g.

Worth watching

  • Fiber dip: intake fell to 14.1 g on Aug 28 and 11.2 g on Aug 29, below the 22.0 g weekly average.
  • Sleep fragmentation: records showed 28–49 awakenings on several nights, with one Aug 28 record at 78.8% efficiency.
  • Late timing: sleep starts clustered around a median of about 1:39 a.m., with one record beginning at 3:16 a.m.
  • Recovery variability: HRV dipped to 31.98 ms on Aug 26 and 32.52 ms on Aug 29; these remain short-term blips until more weeks accumulate.
  • Volume context: exercise reached 619 minutes. There is no clear over-strain signal here, but this week does not call for adding more load.
