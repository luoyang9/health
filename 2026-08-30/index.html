Medical boundary
Wearable data supports pattern recognition but cannot rule conditions in or out. Persistent low-quality sleep, loud snoring, gasping, morning headaches, excessive daytime sleepiness, sustained heart-rate changes, or concerning symptoms belong with a qualified clinician.

Training safety note

Use controlled technique, breathe through effort, and avoid prolonged breath-holding. Any activity-specific guidance from a clinician overrides this report.
