What to keep—and what to watch

Observations from a complete Monday–Sunday window; not a diagnosis.

Working well

  • Steps up a third week: 9,356 per day on average, up from 8,075, with a Sunday high of 15,324 on a hiking day.
  • Movement variety: 30 workouts mixed 16 walks, three hikes, eight table-tennis sessions, and three strength sessions of 41–52 minutes each.
  • Stable core signals: resting heart rate stayed 59–67 bpm, weight measured 66.9–67.7 kg, and VO₂ max rose from 44.55 on Sep 1 to 45.23 on Sep 4.
  • Recovery finished strong: HRV averaged 41.2 ms, the highest weekly reading yet, rebounding to 39.6 ms on Sunday after two lower days.
  • Protein strength: daily intake averaged 170.3 g, with six of seven days at or above 131 g.

Worth watching

  • Strength cadence: three sessions versus the established roughly five-times-weekly routine and seven last week—the week’s clearest cadence drift.
  • Fiber soft most days: intake averaged 18.1 g and stayed under 20 g on five of seven days; only Aug 31 cleared 30 g, at 33.8 g.
  • One short night: Sep 1 logged 5.90 hours from a 3:22 a.m. start (quality 47). The other six nights all reached 7.6 hours or more.
  • HRV tracked load: readings fell to 30.2 ms on Sep 4 and 31.7 ms on Sep 5—the week’s two heaviest days—then rebounded to 39.6 ms by Sunday, which reads as load response rather than a standalone concern.
  • Weight: measured days moved from 66.9 to 67.5 kg. That is fluctuation, not a trend, but it stays in the weekly view.
